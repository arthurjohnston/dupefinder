General Index: checked pairs

Pairs of articles found by this project's scan of the General Index (word n-grams from over 100 million articles), checked against both articles' full text, and reported to the journal concerned, except those marked "not reported" (judged not worth a report, but published so the comparison is public). Each page shows the measured overlap: the shared passages side by side with the differences marked, and the longest one as printed in both articles. "Shared text" is the share of each article's words that fall inside a run of 10 or more matching words (allowing substituted words within a run). A page shows measured text overlap; it does not establish why the overlap exists, and none of these pages is a finding of misconduct by any named person.

Where the publication years differ, the later article is listed first. Where they are the same, which came first is unknown and the order means nothing.
