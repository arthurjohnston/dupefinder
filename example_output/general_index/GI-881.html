In the case of distal pancreatectomy with splenectomy, the splenic artery and splenic vein were divided. The spleen was resected with the pancreas. The specimen was then placed in a plastic retrieval bag and removed through a minimally extended umbilical port incision. Two closed suction drains were placed in the splenic fossa next to the remnant pancreas.
